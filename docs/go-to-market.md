# Go-to-market plan

## Initial offer

Do not sell a broad "AI platform" first. Sell a scoped outcome:

> Help engineering teams safely operate shared AI workloads with tenant controls, release gates, audit evidence, cost visibility, and rollback.

## Pilot format

1. Assess one AI workload and its approval, tenancy, cost, reliability, and security gaps.
2. Implement one narrow governed workflow.
3. Exercise a success and failure path with the customer.
4. Measure a defined result.
5. Convert successful pilots into an annual subscription, self-hosted licence, managed service, or support agreement.

## Commercial models

| Offer | Buyer outcome | Model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosted control plane | Shared governance and operations | Subscription |
| Enterprise self-hosted | Control in regulated/private environments | Annual licence + support |
| Governance pack | Policy, approval, audit, compliance evidence | Enterprise add-on |
| Operations/FinOps add-on | Cost allocation, SLO, capacity and remediation | Usage or workload tier |
| Edge fleet add-on | Signed models, rollout and local/cloud routing | Active-device tier |
| Services | Deployment and policy design | Fixed implementation fee |

## Early channels

- Technical writing based on genuine local evidence.
- Open-source adoption and contributor relationships.
- AI-platform assessments for startups and regulated companies.
- Platform, SRE, MLOps, cloud-native, and AI governance communities.
- Targeted conversations with CTOs, Heads of Platform, Heads of AI/ML, and security leaders.

## Evidence that matters

One external user who can show a reduced-risk release or clearer cost allocation matters more than another feature list.
