# Career and category-leadership plan

> **Historical strategy note.** Superseded on 2026-10-08 by the davdeploy commercial-product decision. Preserved for history.

## Goal

Become the technical leader trusted to define and operate enterprise AI platform capabilities: governance, reliability, delivery, security, observability, cost, and developer experience.

## Near-term target roles

- Staff or Principal AI Platform Engineer
- Founding AI Infrastructure Engineer
- AI Infrastructure / LLMOps Platform Lead
- AI Reliability Engineering Lead
- Platform Engineering Lead, AI
- AI Governance Engineering Lead

## Leadership evidence to develop

- A coherent platform strategy and build-vs-buy framework.
- Adoption and business outcomes, not only technical demos.
- Cross-functional influence with security, legal, finance, data, product, and infrastructure groups.
- Operational leadership: SLOs, incidents, postmortems, capacity, and recovery.
- Mentoring, standards, reusable patterns, and roadmap execution.

## Twelve-month intent

1. Finish and communicate the ten validated reference projects.
2. Build the flagship's narrow MVP, not an oversized monolith.
3. Publish deep technical case studies and give talks/workshops.
4. Obtain external user, design-partner, consulting, or production evidence.
5. Pursue Staff/Principal/founding-lead roles using that operating model.

## Narrative

> I help organisations move from AI experiments to governed, reliable, and cost-aware production systems.
