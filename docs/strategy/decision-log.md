# Decision log

## 2026-09-26 — Preserve focused public projects

The ten portfolio repositories remain independent, public, local-first reference implementations. They are technical evidence and future sources of reusable patterns.

## 2026-09-26 — Build a separate flagship later

After the ten projects are complete, create an integrated flagship from a narrow governed AI gateway and model-release workflow. Do not prematurely merge all existing repositories into a monolith.

## 2026-09-26 — Use open-core boundaries

Keep educational/local reference work public. Keep commercial hosted capabilities, enterprise integrations, customer data, configurations, secrets, and support systems private.

## 2026-09-26 — Pursue a leadership path alongside product optionality

The immediate goal is to become hireable as a Staff/Principal/founding AI platform leader. A future product can emerge through external adoption, design partners, or consulting evidence rather than assuming an immediate startup path.
# 2026-09-27 — Public flagship direction

Use `multi-tenant-ai-inference-platform` as the eventual public flagship and retain
the other nine completed repositories as independent reference implementations. Integrate
validated patterns through narrow contracts and vertical slices; do not merge ten codebases
into a monolith. Commercial strategy remains private to this repository.
# Decision — 2026-10-08: davdeploy becomes the private commercial flagship

**Supersedes, but does not erase, the earlier strategy-only decision below.**

davdeploy is this repository's product name and commercial flagship. `ai-platform-control-plane` is the primary execution and governance ancestor. `multi-tenant-ai-inference-platform` is an important AI-runtime subsystem, not the root product. Public repositories remain portfolio evidence, laboratories and possible future open-source components; proprietary commercial material stays outside them.

The product starts with a local, modular-monolith V1 for AI startups and small engineering teams. It turns an outcome into a typed specification, constrained architecture plan, transparent cost estimate, policy result, immutable plan, approval and bounded observed execution. It does not claim customer-cloud execution until that is run.
