# Packaging hypotheses

These are product hypotheses, not validated prices, promises or customer commitments.

| Package | Intended customer outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Community / Developer | Local learning, examples and future public tooling |
| Startup | Simple self-service planning and a first AWS workload |
| Growth | BYOC, stronger observability and AI-runtime controls |
| Business | SSO/RBAC, policy, audit, release governance and remediation controls |
| Enterprise | Private deployment, multiple accounts/clusters and stronger support/compliance |
| Enterprise+ | Future air-gap, on-premises, custom controls and dedicated support |

Pricing, buyer lists, acquisition hypotheses, customer details and proprietary evaluation data stay private and are not treated as product facts until tested.
