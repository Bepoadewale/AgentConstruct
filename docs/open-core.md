# Open-core and commercial boundaries

## Public / open-source candidates

- Local-first reference implementations and demos.
- SDKs, CLI, sample policy modules, and generic adapters.
- Community edition for a single local or small self-managed deployment.
- Educational documentation and architecture decision records.

## Private / commercial candidates

- Hosted multi-tenant control plane.
- Enterprise SSO/SCIM and advanced role management.
- Managed policy packs and compliance evidence reporting.
- Long-retention audit storage and enterprise administration.
- Production connectors, installation automation, and support tooling.
- Cross-cluster/fleet management, advanced cost optimisation, and proprietary operational analytics.
- Customer configurations, identities, secrets, telemetry, model artefacts, and policies.

## Rule for the current portfolio

Keep the completed portfolio projects public unless a repository contains real customer data, credentials, proprietary code, or a commercial agreement requires otherwise. A public reference implementation creates trust and adoption; it is not the complete product.

Before taking customer data or paid pilots, create a separate private product codebase, choose an appropriate licence with legal advice, establish terms/privacy/security practices, and segregate every customer environment.
