# ADR-008: Keep public references separate

## Context

The public portfolio demonstrates real, focused engineering work.

## Decision

Keep them independent. Bring patterns into davdeploy through clear interfaces and documentation.

## Alternatives

Merge all public repositories into one product monorepo.

## Consequences

Public evidence remains understandable and private commercial information stays private.

## Security and operations

No private customer, pricing or proprietary material is copied into public repositories.
